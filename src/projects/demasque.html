Narrative point-and-click mystery game set during an opulent 19th-century masquerade ball: cross-examine guests, unveil hidden secrets, and unmask the real culprit.

Project Overview

Murder at the Masquerade

Created for the Global Game Jam 2026, Démasqué puts players in the shoes of a royal undercover spy attending a high-society masquerade ball. When the prince is assassinated behind closed doors, you must question attendees, uncover contradictory alibis, and log discoveries in your investigative notebook to serve justice.

[Placeholder: Detail the GGJ 2026 theme, literary inspirations (Agatha Christie / Sherlock Holmes), and branching narrative mechanics.]

Investigation Features

  • Dynamic detective notebook UI
  • Clue-gated branching dialogue nodes
  • Atmospheric period visual and audio design
  • Multiple endings based on your final verdict

Methodology

Conception Steps

From writing the crime intrigue to interactive dialogue scripting.

Step 01

Mystery Plot & Alibi Matrix

Crafting suspect backgrounds, interwoven motives, red herrings, and an airtight timeline to ensure deduction logic remains coherent throughout.

Step 02

Detective UI & Notebook Ergonomics

Designing UI mockups and Unity UI integration: tabbed investigation journal, portraits of masked aristocrats, and readable dialogue boxes.

Step 03

Unity C# Dialogue Engine

Building the dialogue state machine in C#: unlocking responsive dialogue choices when relevant clues are discovered and validating the final accusation.
