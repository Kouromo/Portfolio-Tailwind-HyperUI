Démasqué

Narrative point-and-click mystery game set during an opulent 19th-century masquerade ball: cross-examine guests, unveil hidden secrets, and unmask the real culprit.

Role UI Designer, Gameplay Scripter & Integrator
Duration 48 Hours (Game Jam)
Technologies & Tools
Unity C# UI Design Point & Click Branching Dialogue
Play on itch.io (GGJ 2026)
Atmospheric welcome screen and opulent 19th-century masquerade ball aesthetic

Project Overview

Murder at the Masquerade

Created for the Global Game Jam 2026, Démasqué puts players in the shoes of a royal undercover spy attending a high-society masquerade ball. When the prince is assassinated behind closed doors, you must question attendees, uncover contradictory alibis, and log discoveries in your investigative notebook to serve justice.
