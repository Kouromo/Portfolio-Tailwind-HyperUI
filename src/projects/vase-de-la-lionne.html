Virtual Reconstruction of the Lioness Vase

Virtual reality (VR) experience on Meta Quest 3 showcasing the archaeological heritage of Massalia (6th century BC).

Role Technical Lead & 3D Artist
Duration 6 months (Oct 2025 - May 2026)
Technologies & Tools
Meta Quest 3 Unity Blender Agile / Scrum Git / GitHub
Watch Demo Video

Project Background & Goals

Conducted as part of the AMINJ Master's program, this project aims to resurrect a 6th-century BC archaeological treasure from Massalia: the Lioness Vase. The core mission was to build a scientifically accurate, standalone virtual reality experience on Meta Quest 3, enabling museum visitors to interactively inspect and manipulate the vase.
