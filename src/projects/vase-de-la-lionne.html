Virtual Reconstruction of the Lioness Vase

Virtual reality (VR) experience on Meta Quest 3 showcasing the archaeological heritage of Massalia (6th century BC).

Role Level Artist & Scrum Master
Duration 3 months (February 2026 - May 2026)
Technologies & Tools
Meta Quest 3 Unity Blender Photopea Agile / Scrum Git / GitHub

Project Background & Goals

Conducted as part of the AMINJ Master's program, this tutored project aims to bring a 6th-century BC archaeological masterpiece from Massalia back to life: the Lioness Vase.
The initial objective was to create an immersive virtual reality experience highlighting the cultural and historical significance of the vase on Meta Quest 3.

A wide range of resources was gathered: archaeological research papers, sketches of how the vase originally looked, photographs of a ceramic replica crafted by a master potter, high-definition captures of the original artifact, and site photos from the excavation at Place des Pistoles.

Key Objectives

  • Archaeological heritage popularization
  • VR ergonomics & anti-motion sickness comfort
  • Agile / Scrum team management
  • Accessibility (guided tutorials)
  • Creation of an immersive spatialized soundscape
  • Historical fidelity and scale accuracy to the original vase

Methodology

Design & Development Steps

Step 1

Research & Preparatory Study

Analysis of archaeological data and reference documents. Drafting specifications, scoping the sprint backlog, and organizing task management under Scrum methodology.

Step 2

UV Unwrapping & High-Fidelity Texturing

While the 3D mesh was modeled by my teammate Loïc Pauvert, I handled the UV unwrapping and the creation of high-resolution textures.

A meticulous and time-intensive reconstruction effort based on analyzing artifact photos, sketches, the potter's physical model, and discussions with our academic advisor to faithfully restore the vase's fine details.

Step 3

Unity Integration & Historical Ecosystem

Integrating the textured vase into Unity and reconstructing an immersive period environment.

This work began with in-depth research into the Mediterranean biome of 6th-century BC Massalia (native flora, topography, climate). I then curated, calibrated, and imported cohesive 3D assets to build an authentic natural landscape, creating a believable and engaging ecosystem that enriches the virtual tour experience.

Step 4

Audio, Narration & Final Defense

Finalizing immersion by integrating a comprehensive soundscape (natural ambiences) and implementing a voice-over narrator guiding the user through the history of the vase throughout the experience.

This phase also encompassed VR comfort adjustments and preparing presentation materials for the final project defense.
