Project Overview

Interactive AI Training for Orange

Designed in partnership with Orange during our Master's degree, DayLIA addresses the challenge of making artificial intelligence accessible and demystifying it for enterprise collaborators. Through bite-sized interactive scenarios and situational challenges, employees experience prompt engineering, bias detection, and source verification firsthand. An automated feedback module guides users step-by-step through their learning curve.
