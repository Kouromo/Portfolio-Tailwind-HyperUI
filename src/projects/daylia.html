DayLIA

Interactive training tool for Orange professionals on the use and acculturation of Artificial Intelligence.

Role Unity Developer & UX Designer
Duration 1 month
Technologies & Tools
C# Unity UX/UI Design IA WebGL
Test the project online GitHub Repository Figma Moodboard

Context & Training Objectives

Developed in an educational context with a mock client from Telecom acting as the Director of Innovation, DayLIA addresses the acculturation of teams to the daily use of generative and predictive artificial intelligence for professional tasks.

Through a series of decision-making mini-games, various professional profiles are placed in real-world situations (writing prompts, detecting biases, verifying sources). An automated feedback module simulating an AI guides the user step-by-step in their progress.

This approach was carried out following an Agile Scrum methodology, with weekly sprints and client reviews. It was also necessary to calculate project costs to produce a realistic invoice.

Pedagogical Pillars

  • Micro-learning & quick sessions (5-10 min)
  • Interactive feedback & contextualized explanations
  • Cross-platform WebGL deployment
  • Corporate & engaging graphic charter

Methodology

Design Stages

From gathering user needs to online deployment.

Step 1

Needs Analysis & UX Design

Definition of professional personas, wireframing, and creation of the interactive moodboard in Figma.

Defined personas include salespeople, customer advisors, technicians, and managers.

Step 2

Interface Design in Unity

Development of mini-games and user interactions, integration of graphic assets, and animation of UI elements.

Adherence to the defined graphic charter

Step 3

Linking Elements

Linking the interface with the backend for user login and using APIs for generating feedback and tracking scores.

Step 4

Deployment and delivery

Project deployment, delivery of the organizational document, and presentation of the invoice to the client.

Project Review

DayLIA demonstrated how an educational game can defuse apprehensions surrounding artificial intelligence and encourage its informed adoption in the workplace.

In this project, I was able to work on design, agile organization via a Kanban and following a Roadmap, creation of interactive interfaces (especially on mobile).
