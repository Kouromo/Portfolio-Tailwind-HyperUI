Scenario & Infiltration Mechanics

In Impact Certificate (Space Spies), the player takes on the role of an alien spy infiltrating an advanced human research facility focused on immersive technologies.

To progress without arousing the suspicion of patrols and cameras, the agent must solve technical puzzles and interactive crossword grids covering the fundamental concepts of virtual reality (latency, 6DoF tracking, field of view FOV, stereoscopic display).



This game is fully translated into Spanish as it is intended for Colombian students

Gameplay Pillars

  • Infiltration & patrol vision cones
  • VR didactic puzzles & crosswords
  • Stylized low-poly spatial 3D environment
  • Story-driven progression in chapters

This game improved my 3D modeling skills, and I was able to create gameplay mechanics such as the interactive crossword in Unity.
