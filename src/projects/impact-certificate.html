Scenario & Stealth Mechanics

In Impact Certificate (Space Spies), players infiltrate a human advanced research center studying immersive technologies as an extraterrestrial operative.

To navigate undetected around guards and security cameras, the agent must solve technical puzzles and interactive crosswords covering core VR concepts (tracking latency, 6DoF movement, field of view, stereoscopy).
