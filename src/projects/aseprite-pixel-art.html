Pixel Art & Sprites (Aseprite)

Creation of retro illustrations, tilesets and frame by frame animation cycles for 2D video games in Aseprite with slicing into spritesheets.

Role Pixel Artist & 2D Animator
Duration Continuous practice
Technologies & Tools
Aseprite Indexed palettes 2D Animation Hue Shifting Spritesheets

Creative Approach

Visual Pixel art

Pixel art is the art of making every pixel meaningful by working on constrained resolutions (often 16x16, 32x32 or 64x64 sizes).

I was very inspired by games like Minecraft, Stardew Valley, Terraria.
