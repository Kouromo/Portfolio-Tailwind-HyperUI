Creative Philosophy

The Art of Visual Constraint

Pixel art is the craft of making every pixel carry weight. Operating within strict resolution ceilings (16x16, 32x32, or 64x64) demands crisp silhouette readability, disciplined value contrast, and thoughtful palette selection to give life and personality to 2D game worlds.
