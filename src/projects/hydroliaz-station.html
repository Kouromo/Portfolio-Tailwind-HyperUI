Hydroliaz Station

Serious game developed in Unity (C#) to teach students about the states and transformations of water in a fun and interactive way.

Concept & Educational Objectives

At the heart of an intergalactic hydration space station, the player takes on the role of a cosmic bartender who must serve demanding alien clients. Each alien species comes from a planet with extreme climatic and atmospheric conditions.

To succeed, the player must manipulate temperature, pressure, and containers to transform water into the correct physical state (solid, liquid, or gas) while understanding the underlying thermodynamic principles.

The game targets 15-year-old students following the 10th-grade physics-chemistry curriculum. The goal is for them to memorize the different states of water and the names of the transformations.

Pedagogical Mechanics

  • State changes: melting, vaporization, condensation
  • Interactive temperature & pressure parameters
  • Progressive validation via score & customer satisfaction
  • Clear and accessible visual feedback

Design

Creation Stages

From didactic transposition to Unity implementation.

Step 1

Didactic Transposition & Game Design

Translating physics and chemistry concepts (saturation curves, phase changes) into dynamic and rewarding gameplay loops.

In the game, there are three types of aliens: the cloud which consumes water vapor, the orc which consumes ice, and the fish which consumes liquid water.

Step 2

Unity Project & State Management

Implementation of a machine that determines the state of water based on the climate. The player can transform and deliver it to the aliens. As time passes, the climate changes, forcing the player to adapt to serve the correct drink.

Step 3

User Interface & Visual Feedback

Design of a clear interface and a book serving as a pedagogical guide to help the player understand water transformations and the necessary conditions for each state.
