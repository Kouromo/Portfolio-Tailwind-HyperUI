Fast-paced 2D typing roguelike where your keyboard acts as a magic wand to cast spells, dodge enemy attacks, and conquer hostile dungeons.

Project Overview

When Typing Meets Action Roguelikes

Keyzard reimagines traditional educational typing games by combining them with top-down action roguelike adrenaline. Developed solo in Unity (C#), this project enabled me to build a zero-latency keystroke matching engine while authoring the entire pixel art visual identity from scratch.
