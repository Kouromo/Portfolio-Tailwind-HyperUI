Lost Labyrinth

Tense maze escape survival game developed in 48 hours, where the player must navigate an eerie labyrinth while avoiding a relentless roaming entity.

Role Game Designer & Gameplay C# Developer
Duration 48 Hours (Game Jam)

Project Overview

Spatial Tension & Urgency

Crafted during Euskajam 2023, Lost Labyrinth was my second collaborative game jam experience. Responding to the prompt, our team chose to experiment with psychological survival horror: depriving players of reliable mapping tools, amplifying audio cues, and unleashing an unpredictable threat.

Survival Pillars

  • Zero minimap / pure spatial memory
  • Stalker AI with acoustic detection
  • 3D spatial sound to locate danger
  • Stamina and heartbeat tension pacing
