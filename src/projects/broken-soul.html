Broken Soul

2D action-adventure video game developed as a team during my very first Game Jam, completed under strict time constraints.

Role Game Designer & Pixel Artist
Duration 48 Hours (Game Jam)
Technologies & Tools
Unity Git / GitHub Photoshop 2D Gameplay
Play on itch.io
Broken Soul title screen

Project Context

Reverse platformer

Broken Soul represents the creation of my very first video game as part of the WinterJam organized by the Hego Joko student association. The goal of this game is to figure out how to die to advance to the next level.

The theme of the Jam was "Broken World"

Key Challenge Points

  • Fast creation of pixel assets and animations for the game
  • Organization of ideas and tasks for a team project
  • Level design

Experience Summary

This first game jam was a formative catalyst: learning to deliver a finished project in 48 hours, communicating under pressure, and making compromises.

Even though we didn't finish first, I am very happy that our team won second place and this game has a very special place in my heart. The game just lacks SFX to enhance the game's atmosphere.
